Hi, I am Sanket

I am a B.Sc. Mathematics Honours student from a village. In 9th class, I met some defence aspirants at our local ground. Their energy inspired me, and I made NDA my goal.

I failed the NDA written exam 5 times. My failures had different reasons: low preparation, a fever before the exam, and silly mistakes under pressure. I did not quit. I changed my method. In April 2026, on my 6th and last attempt, I cleared the written exam. Then I attended the SSB at 22 SSB Bhopal.

This page is for students who study on their own. I share plans, trackers and templates in simple words. I cannot promise selection. I can share what helped me.

Smart work

Ten hours of study is useless if you make silly mistakes. Mind maps, a mistake log and focused revision changed my game.

Calm mind

When I was desperate to win, I failed. When I dropped my expectations and focused on the paper, I cleared it.

Consistency

Steady daily work beats 14 hours and burnout. I balanced defence prep and college exams this way.
